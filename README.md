# My NeuroAI & Health study tracker

Just a small personal project — a self-made tracker for a month I set aside to read up on how neuroscience and AI have influenced each other, and where that overlaps with healthcare and nursing/midwifery education. Sharing it here in case it's useful to anyone else doing something similar.

## What's in it

One HTML file, no install needed:

- 4 weeks, each with a reading list and a few things to focus on per day
- A little progress tracker (30 days) that saves in your browser
- Checkboxes to mark off my own weekly write-ups

## How to open it

Just open `neuroai-health-tracker.html` in your browser. That's it.

## Note

Progress only saves on the device/browser you use it in. Some of the linked articles may be paywalled depending on your access.
